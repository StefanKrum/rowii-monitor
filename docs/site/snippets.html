Model-flagged, unverified candidates

On days WITHOUT any induced anomaly, the detector still occasionally scores a window as anomalous against its own learned normal model. Every clip below is model-flagged and unverified — a qualitative listening candidate, not a confirmed fault. Alarms fire on two independent paths: a sustained anomaly (at least 3 consecutive 1 s windows with p < 0.01 against the operating mode's normal model, fusion representation) or a transient one (a single window with p < 0.001, BEATs representation). The exact trigger reason is stated under each clip. The 10 shown here are the 5 strongest (lowest minimum p-value) of each class across every measurement day.
