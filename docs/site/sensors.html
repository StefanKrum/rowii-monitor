As-built vs. planned. An earlier planning draft specified a different microphone-ring layout (5 positions per ring, 72° spacing). The geometry shown here is the as-installed configuration verified on site: 4 microphones at 90° spacing on the generator ring, 4 at 90° spacing plus one additional bottom microphone on the turbine ring, and one tri-axial accelerometer pair (0°/180°) per level. The discrepancy between the planned and as-built ring layout is not reconciled in any delivered specification. Separately: the current detection pipeline consumes one mono channel per audio stream (channel 0 of RAWGeneratorMic__0 / RAWTurbineMic__1); the full multi-microphone geometry shown here is the physical installation, not (yet) a per-channel model input.
